# Amass City — Game Design Document

> Original IP. Genre-inspired by open-world action games. Not a clone, port, or
> derivative of any existing copyrighted title. All names, characters, places,
> and story beats are original.

## 1. High Concept

A third-person open-world action game set in **Amass City**, a fictional
coastal metropolis on the fictional Solara Coast. The player controls
**Reza Marin**, a former mechanic dragged back into the city's underworld
after a family tragedy. The game blends sandbox freedom (driving, exploration,
emergent chaos) with a directed narrative campaign, side activities, and a
dynamic crime/police response system.

## 2. Pillars

1. **Sandbox first.** Every system (driving, combat, AI) must work standalone
   in the open world before it works inside a scripted mission.
2. **Honest physics.** Vehicles use Chaos. Damage, weight, and traction matter.
3. **Reactive city.** NPCs and police respond to what the player does, not to
   scripted triggers.
4. **Personal story, big city.** Reza's arc is small and specific even as the
   city is vast.

## 3. Setting

**Amass City** is a 36 km² fictional metropolis split into:

| District       | Theme                                  |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Harbor Flats   | Working-class waterfront, cargo, docks |
| Veridian Hill  | Wealthy hilltop estates                |
| The Loop       | Downtown core, banks, towers           |
| Mosaic Row     | Cultural / market district             |
| Northpoint     | Industrial / refineries                |
| The Outlands   | Rural fringe, oil fields, badlands     |

Surrounding terrain: ocean to the south, badlands to the north, two highway
arteries connecting districts.

## 4. Core Loop

Drive → Explore → Encounter (mission, side activity, crime) → Escalate
(combat, chase) → Escape or resolve → Reward (cash, reputation, gear) → Repeat.

## 5. Key Systems

### 5.1 Player Character (`AmassCharacter`)
- Third-person; walk, sprint, jump, climb-low, take cover.
- Health via `HealthComponent` (regenerating last segment).
- Vehicle interaction via radius scan + `Interact`.

### 5.2 Vehicles (`AmassVehicleBase`)
- Chaos Wheeled Vehicle base. Per-class tunings stored in DataAssets:
  - **Compact** — agile, low top speed.
  - **Sedan** — balanced.
  - **Sport** — high top speed, fragile.
  - **SUV / Truck** — heavy, off-road.
  - **Motorcycle** (deferred to phase 3) — single-track physics.
- Damage model affects engine/handling, not just visuals.

### 5.3 Wanted Level (`WantedLevelComponent`)
- 0–5 stars. Crimes add heat; heat decays after a delay if no new crime.
- At each level, a new police behavior unlocks (patrol → pursuit → roadblocks
  → helicopter → SWAT). All policing is fictional and stylized.

### 5.4 Missions (`UMissionSystem`, world subsystem)
- DataAsset-driven: id, display name, description, requirements, reward.
- States: Locked → Available → Active → Completed/Failed.
- Hooks into Gameplay Tags so designers can author requirements declaratively.

### 5.5 Time of Day (`AmassGameState`)
- Real seconds → in-world minutes via `MinutesPerRealSecond`.
- Phase events (Dawn / Day / Dusk / Night) drive lighting, NPC density, and
  certain mission availability.

### 5.6 NPCs (`AmassNPCCharacter`)
- Roles: Civilian, Police, Vendor, Hostile.
- AI is a behavior tree per role; civilians use crowd flow with goal points
  generated by PCG.

### 5.7 HUD (`AAmassHUD`)
- Wanted stars (top-right).
- Speedometer when in vehicle.
- Minimap placeholder (UMG widget upgrade in phase 2).

## 6. Story (one-line beats, no spoilers)

Reza returns to Amass City for his brother's funeral, finds a pawn ticket,
chases it through three crews, learns the city changed while he was gone, and
must choose between leaving again or rebuilding what's left. ~25 main missions
across 4 acts, ~40 side activities.

## 7. Cast (originals, names randomly composed)

- **Reza Marin** — protagonist, ex-mechanic.
- **Ines Calder** — fixer, runs a body shop in Harbor Flats.
- **Detective Park** — APD homicide, the closest thing Reza has to an ally
  inside the system.
- **"Mantis"** — leader of the Mosaic Row crew.
- **Vance Holroyd** — Veridian Hill developer with too-clean hands.

## 8. Art Direction

- Sun-bleached coastal palette by day, neon-saturated by night.
- Architecture: 1970s low-rise + brutalist civic + glass downtown.
- Vehicles: invented brands (e.g., **Korvath**, **Stellan**, **Hansa**) with
  silhouettes inspired by general categories (compact, sedan, sport),
  **never** copying real-world or in-game models.

## 9. Audio

- Adaptive driving music with stems per district.
- Diegetic radio: original tracks commissioned, original DJ banter.
- Foley: city ambience layered by district (docks, traffic, suburbs).

## 10. Out-of-Scope (explicit non-goals)

- No real-world brands, logos, or trademarks.
- No assets, characters, dialogue, map regions, vehicle designs, or radio
  content from any existing game. All references in this repo are to genre
  conventions, not specific titles.
- No online persistent multiplayer in v1. Co-op (2P drop-in) deferred to v1.5.
